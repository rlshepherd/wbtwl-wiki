Skabatha kidnaps children and forces to make toys from her workshop inside [[Loomlurch]].

[[Will of the Wild]] has a plan to rescue them, and the player's agreed. 

(While in Loomlurch, they plan to [[Steal a portrait for Bavlorna]])


### Will's Plan: 

**The Approach.** The [[Getaway Gang]] (Bobi, Sloane, Zennor, and Star) will crepp through the woods to the west of the tree and hife just out of sight of the screaming scarecrows. Meanwhile, [[Will of the Wild]] will sneak around to the entrance to the kitchen on the east side of the tree. 

**The Distraction.** The characters will enter the goblin market posing as travelers, purchase some sweets, and arrange for a meeting with the hag. During the meeting, the children will alert the screaming scarecrows and thereby lure the tin solider into the garden. 

**The Escape.** While everyone is distracted, the characters will free the captive from the workshop while Will climbs into the textile mill to free the captives there. Once the children are set free, everyone will run back to [[Little Oak]].
