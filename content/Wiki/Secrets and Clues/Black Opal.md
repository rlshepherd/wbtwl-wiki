
This opal originally belonged to [[Lyra's family]]. She stole it, along with some ordinary jewels, during her unceremonious exit from Waterdeep. 

Unbeknowst to her, this was no ordinary black opal. It has some connection with Zybilna and the Prismeer.  

Lyra's family hired the Wild Hunt to hunt down her and Steven to retrieve the Opal, and have been harrying the party.

Later, the Black Opal accompanied Steven while he was trapped in another plane. He, and the opal, came back some changed, carrying the touch of unknown power.
