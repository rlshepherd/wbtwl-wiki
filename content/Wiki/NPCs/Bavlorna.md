A Hag of [[The Hourglass Coven]]. 

Rules over [[Hither]] from her hut in [[Downfall]]. 

She is allergic to seeing people run widdershins.

Stole [[Lyra]]'s sense of direction (literal and metaphorical) during her visit to the [[The Witchlight Carnival]]. 

The characters completed some chores for Bavlorna, to earn her favor and get a chance to request for Lyra's sense of direction back. 

The characters have agreed to [[Steal a portrait for Bavlorna]] from her sister [[Skabatha]]'s home in [[Loomlurch]]. 

On their way out of Bavlorna's hut, they stole a [[Mysterious Hourglass]] from a bedroom on the second floor.
