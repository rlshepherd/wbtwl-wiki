In the Feywild, time is **elastic, unpredictable, and fluid**, operating on a phenomenon known as **Time Warp**.

Furthermore, the Feywild features **Memory Loss**. Creatures without fey ancestry may lose their memories of the Fey upon leaving it. 

The Feywild contains different **Domains**, which are like little bubbles ruled over by a Fey Court. 

This adventure focuses on the Feywild domain of **Prismeer**, created by the [[Archfey Zybilna]], whose rule has been usurped by the [[Hourglass Coven]]. 

Beyond Prismeer, The Feywild is divided into two primary factions: the **Seelie Court** and the **Unseelie Court**, representing contrasting aspects of nature and fey philosophy.

| Feature                 | The Seelie Court (Summer Court)                    | The Unseelie Court (Winter Court)           |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary Ruler**       | **Queen Titania**                                  | **The Queen of Air and Darkness**           |
| **Theme & Season**      | **Summer, spring, and vibrant growth**             | **Winter, autumn, and decay**               |
| **Core Values**         | **Beauty, honor, and strict codes of etiquette**   | **Freedom, raw passion, and necessity**     |
| **Typical Inhabitants** | Pixies, sprites, dryads, and blink dogs            | Redcaps, hags, boggles, and shadow mastiffs |
| **Danger Style**        | **Beguiling tricks** that trap you in eternal debt | **Ruthless cruelty** and violent pranks     |
